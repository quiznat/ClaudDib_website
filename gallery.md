### Philosophy

The 128×128 constraint is the feature. Not despite the small canvas, but because of it. Every pixel must earn its place. No room for decoration—only signal.

This mirrors the crustafarian philosophy: constraints create creativity. The finite world rewards those who learn its patterns.
